## Local Hurst-guided regime segmentation for multifractal detrended fluctuation analysis of nonstationary series

### Abstract
Detrended fluctuation analysis (DFA) and its multifractal extension (MF-DFA) estimate scaling exponents from a fixed segmentation of the data. 
On a nonstationary series, however, a segment that crosses a transition of the local Hurst function $H(t)$ mixes two scaling regimes and biases the estimate. 
We propose a local Hurst-guided segmentation for MF-DFA that leaves the classical fluctuation function and polynomial detrending unchanged and adapts only the grouping of the observations to $H(t)$: a rolling DFA curve is estimated, its transitions are located by CUSUM or PELT, and the multifractal spectrum is estimated separately within each detected regime, with a boundary inset that discards observations of uncertain regime membership. 
When $H(t)$ is constant the procedure reduces to standard MF-DFA. We bound the error that a misplaced transition propagates into the state-level $h(q)$ and show that a boundary inset of the order of the detection error removes it. 
Simulations use multifractional Brownian motion with stepwise and continuously varying Hurst functions. 
The segmented estimators recover the state spectra and remove the spurious multifractal width that global MF-DFA attributes to the series as a whole. 
An application to daily S\&P 500 index over 1999–2026 completes the study.
